# QA/QC pass 5 — methane leakage dashboard and international data (November 17, 2026)

Independent review. Raw files were re-read and aggregates recomputed; the page was driven in headless Chrome at 1200 px and 400 px.

## 1. Bottom line

The page is now clean on everything I could test, but it was not clean when I started. Six real errors were on the page or in the workbook; all are fixed in the local build. The companion Sheet, the README/DATA_LICENSES and the `public/` package still need the changes listed in sections 3 and 6.

What changed in this pass:

- **Large-leak counts by country were wrong in the "Large leaks since 2024" view.** Each 1° square was given to the country at its centre point, so border and coastal plumes moved country (Mexico showed 177, true 96; Kuwait 31, true 88; Turkmenistan 1,701, true 1,979; "Offshore" 759, true 214 at sea). The view also disagreed with the country panel on the same page. Plumes are now counted one by one.
- **Five countries showed "0% of NaN sites"** in the UN alerts panel (Syria, Yemen, Jordan, Italy, South Africa).
- **Pride Mine (Kentucky) carried a Utah mine's coal output and status** on the US coal layer and in the Sheet (53.9 t methane per kt coal; correct 5.0).
- **The Sheet's "UNEP ÷ EPA 2023" column is blank for every mine** and the year column shows "2024.0x" (formula and formats pointed one column left).
- One probable wrong UNEP-to-EPA mine match was dropped (Crooked Creek paired with Parkwood, a separate MSHA mine).
- Smaller display and wording fixes (list below).

The US cost model, its outputs and the US page text are unchanged since QA pass 4 and still agree with each other. No model errors found.

No console errors in any view before or after. No horizontal overflow at 400 px on any tab.
