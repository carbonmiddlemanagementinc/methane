# QA/QC pass 6: whole page, November 17, 2026

Five independent reviewers, none of whom built the page, each took one area:

1. EU import rules: inputs and maths.
2. EU import rules: importer reach, fee scenarios, the coal table and the crude-by-field table.
3. Global Methane Map.
4. The US cost model and the three US tabs.
5. The companion Sheet, the public package and the page's rendering.

They re-derived inputs from the raw files, re-ran the builds, recomputed every headline, checked the regulation and policy claims online and drove every tab in a headless browser. The builder checked each High and Medium finding against its source before changing anything. This was the first independent review of the EU import rules tab, which was added after pass 5.

## Bottom line

- **US cost model unchanged.** The pipeline re-ran from its inputs with zero differences, and every US headline stands: $2.7 billion a year, last tonne about $1,400, 1.55% baseline, fee of $1,211 reaching 0.52%, WEC as written 1.36%.
- **One input error, on the EU tab.** Guyana's 13.0 Mt of crude was missing from the Eurostat download, and 11.8 Mt of crude with no named origin had been dropped (gas with no named origin was kept). Both are now in. A sum check in the build stops it if the partners do not add up to Eurostat's total.
- **EU headlines after the fix.**
  - EU oil and gas imports: 29.7 EJ (was 28.6), with crude 470 Mt (was 445).
  - Methane in those imports: 5.3 Mt (was 5.2).
  - Share of imports from suppliers above 0.2%: 74% on the IEA estimate (was 75%), 58% on the satellite estimate (was 64%).
  - Oil and gas sold to the EU, Japan and South Korea: 7.4 Mt of methane, 9.2% of the world's oil and gas methane (was 7.3 Mt, 9.0%).
  - The fee results at $1,500 are unchanged to one decimal.
- **The 0.2% benchmark was on the wrong basis.** The 0.2% discussed for the EU is a share of gas by volume, about 38 g/GJ on this page's basis. The page had used 36 g/GJ, which is 0.2% of energy at methane's gross heating value. The slider and markers now use 38 and 75 g/GJ.
- **Everything else was framing and disclosure.** Titles that overstated what the data show, caveats that named only one direction of error, labels that did not say where a number comes from, and missing sources and licences. All are corrected below.
- **Rendering.** All six tabs were checked at 1,200 and 400 px, in light and dark mode, with every view and setting. There were no console errors and no page overflow.
